# Meridian sources

*A short, hand-picked list. This is the default. It is not a giant scrape.*

Meridian reads these sources every week, then an editor (or the weekly compile) picks five to seven stories. Signal sources can help spot a story. They are never cited alone.

| Source | Role | Place | Why it is on the list |
|---|---|---|---|
| OpenAI News | core | US | Official launches and policy. |
| Google DeepMind Blog | core | UK / US | Gemini and research notes. |
| Anthropic News | core | US | Stand-in feed; Anthropic has no official RSS. |
| Mistral AI News | core | EU | The main European open-model lab. |
| Hugging Face lab releases | core | CN, JP, KR, IN, SEA | Model drops from labs with no public blog. |
| Rest of World | core | Global South | Tech outside the usual US-Europe beat. |
| SCMP Tech | core | HK, CN | Hong Kong paper of record for China and HK tech. |
| KrASIA | core | CN, SEA | China and Southeast Asia tech business. |
| Tech in Asia | core | SEA | Southeast Asia startups and AI. |
| Recode China AI | core | CN | Chinese AI companies and strategy. |
| Hacker News (Algolia) | signal only | Global | A cross-check. Missing HN points never lower a story. |

Machine-readable copy: `sources.csv`. Seed with `npm run db:seed-sources`. Adding a source is a curation choice, not a crawl.
